Data

NHL EDGE puck and player tracking, 10 positions a second, for every Ovechkin goal since 2023-24 that has a tracking replay. Goal details come from the NHL play-by-play. Fan-made, not affiliated with the NHL or the Washington Capitals.

How the moments are found

The puck counts as on a player's stick within 6.5 feet of his tracked position. The shot is the last frame on Ovechkin's stick; the pass is the last teammate before him. A one-timer is 0.1 seconds or less on his stick. The detected passer matches the official first assist on goals.

Limits

Ten frames a second blurs anything shorter than a tenth of a second, so speeds are averages over the flight and read lower than radar-gun release speeds. Goalie speed is the fastest he moved between frames while the shot was in the air.
